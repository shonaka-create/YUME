I work solo as your photographer. A round-trip travel fee from Tokyo Station will be billed separately — thank you for your understanding.

Shooting Location

Street shoots in parks and public spaces come at no extra location cost. For special locations such as Tokyo Tower or amusement parks, you'll need to arrange the booking and cover any entry fees yourself. I'm also happy to help you choose a location together based on where you live — just let me know.

Style & Editing

From pre-wedding portraits to photos suited for opening or profile videos, I shoot to match your vision — natural smiles, a relaxed mood, cinematic styling, or the soft, elegant look of Korean wedding photography. I'm glad to suggest poses on the day too. Sharing reference images ahead of time (and meeting beforehand, if convenient) helps me capture exactly what you're picturing. High-resolution delivery is also available.

Outfits

Please arrange your own outfits for the shoot. Depending on the date, an assistant may be able to join to help with luggage and light support, but please note they are not able to provide hair, makeup, or dressing assistance.

Delivery

  • 30+ photos delivered (at least 5 retouched)
  • Format: JPEG
  • Delivered via Gigafile便 (large file transfer)
  • Delivered within 1–2 weeks of the shoot

Video Recording

I don't have professional videography skills, so video quality may not meet expectations for a polished result. I can shoot simple video from angles you specify using an iPhone, but please understand the quality will be closer to something a friend might capture. For a proper video production, I'd recommend booking a dedicated videographer.
